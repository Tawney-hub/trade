A Global Business Matching Company

Trade Match is a global exhibitions, events, and trade Global Business Matching Company focused on creating strategic business linkages between international markets and emerging economies.

  • We specialise in building world-class trade platforms that connect manufacturers, suppliers, investors, buyers, governments, and industry leaders.
  • Trade Match serves as a bridge between Africa and the global marketplace, facilitating international business engagement and cross-border trade relationships.
  • Our experience includes supporting major international exhibitions such as China Trade Fair and China HomeLife, enabling international exhibitors to connect with African markets.

We are expanding our partnerships and exhibition footprint across China, Germany, the Middle East, South Korea, Japan, Vietnam, and other international markets.

Manufacturing, Automotive & Industrial Equipment

Connecting global manufacturers with buyers and distributors across African and emerging markets.

Trade Match facilitates strategic linkages between international manufacturers and African importers, distributors, and procurement bodies. Our exhibitions provide a platform to showcase machinery, automotive products, and industrial equipment to qualified buyers.

Technology & Business Services

Bridging the technology gap between global innovators and Africa's fast-growing digital economy.

From ICT and fintech to SaaS and business services, our trade platforms connect technology companies with government agencies, enterprises, and distributors seeking cutting-edge solutions across Africa and emerging markets.

Construction & Infrastructure

Supporting Africa's infrastructure boom by connecting builders, developers, and suppliers.

As Africa's construction sector expands rapidly, Trade Match provides exhibitions and matchmaking platforms that connect international suppliers of building materials, construction equipment, and infrastructure solutions with developers and government procurement.

Energy & Logistics Supply Chain

Facilitating partnerships in Africa's critical energy and logistics sectors.

Trade Match connects international energy companies, renewable solution providers, and logistics operators with African buyers, utilities, and governments driving infrastructure investment. Our events create the space for deals that power the continent forward.

Consumer Goods, Retail & Home Lifestyle

Bringing global consumer brands to Africa's growing middle-class market.

From home furnishings and lifestyle products to FMCG and retail, our exhibitions, including China HomeLife, open doors to Africa's consumer markets for international brands and help local retailers source competitive products directly from global manufacturers.

China Trade Fair

One of our flagship exhibition partnerships connecting Chinese manufacturers directly with African buyers across multiple industries.

  • Direct access to thousands of verified Chinese manufacturers and exporters.
  • Structured buyer matching programmes and on-site business meetings.
  • Products spanning manufacturing, electronics, construction, and consumer goods.

Trade Match facilitates end-to-end participation for African buyers and assists international exhibitors in navigating market entry across Southern and sub-Saharan Africa.

China HomeLife

An internationally acclaimed home and lifestyle trade show that Trade Match has supported in connecting exhibitors with African retail and wholesale markets.

  • Covering home furnishings, décor, kitchenware, and lifestyle products.
  • Bringing international home brands directly to African retailers and importers.
  • Full event support including stands, media, and buyer coordination.

Our role spans exhibition infrastructure, stakeholder engagement, and buyer programme management — enabling seamless connections between global suppliers and local markets.

Future Exhibition Portfolio

Trade Match is developing its own branded portfolio of exhibitions and international trade platforms across Africa and beyond.

  • Expanding into Germany, the Middle East, South Korea, Japan, and Vietnam.
  • Building Africa-focused exhibitions in manufacturing, energy, and technology.
  • Long-term vision to establish Trade Match as a globally recognised exhibition brand.

Our growth strategy centres on deepening trade and investment relations between Africa and global markets through our own events, conferences, and strategic trade platforms.
